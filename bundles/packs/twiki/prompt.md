## TWiki

TWiki topics are `documentation_page` records with ids
`twiki:<Web>:<Topic>`; inspect one directly when the topic name is known.
Topic names are WikiWords. CMS analysis ids look like `<PAG>-<YY>-<NNN>`,
and topic names drop the hyphens (`<PAG><YY><NNN>`), so search the joined
form too. Cite a topic by its web and name.
