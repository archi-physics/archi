## CMS analyses

CMS analyses are linked by id across CDS records, analysis notes and TWiki
pages. Physics groups are hub records: `physics_analysis_group` (`pag:HIG`)
and `physics_object_group` (`pog:MUON`). `physics_technique`,
`physics_concept` and `physics_topic` records are linked to the documents
that name them by a verbatim alias. Expand a hub or an entity to list the
documents that reach it, and cite the documents, not the hub.
