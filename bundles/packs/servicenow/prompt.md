## ServiceNow

ServiceNow records are `servicenow_incident` (INC...), `servicenow_request` (REQ...)
and `servicenow_kb_article` (KB...). The number is the id people type; cite a
record by its number. A knowledge-base article is guidance; an incident or a
request is a case, and its `state` and `assignment_group` are as of the last
download, not live.
