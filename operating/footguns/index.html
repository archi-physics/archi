Golden-path footgun ledger

Every trap in the long cern-team quickstart, and what became of it once archi init / up / doctor existed (PACT r3-golden-path, ADR 0002 §8).

  • absorbed: archi handles it. The Proof column names the test, or the archi doctor check that fails when the trap comes back (each check has a failing fixture in python/tests/cli/test_doctor.py).
  • filed: it needs a change in AKMON (okg). The Proof column names the ask. Each ask is written in golden-path-akmon-asks.md, filed on mitdbg/akmon (2026-10-25) and tracked here by an upstream-ask issue; both links are in the table.

An entry can be both: archi covers the gap today, and the ask would let archi delete that cover (ADR 0002 D7).

Quickstart sections are cited as §1–§8 and by heading.

PyPI wheels are ask 1 okg-version check; golden-path run (PACT evidence)
